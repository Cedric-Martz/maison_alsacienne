Events and celebrations

2026 programme

Seasonal events at the Alsatian House Park.

21 March — Spring cleaning

Spring cleaning inside and outside the museum, followed by a shared lunch.

28 March — Easter traditions

Games and activities about Alsatian Easter traditions for children and adults.

1 May — Concert

Aperitif, tarte flambée and concert by Musique Union Reichstett, 11 am–12:30 pm.

9–10 May

Traditional mirabelle distillation on 9 May; annual museum trip to the Doubs on 10 May.

6 July–25 August — International workcamps

Rebuilding the 1554 Buschwiller House, rendering, painting and restoring historic objects.

12–19 September

Associations village in Strasbourg, followed by the association’s annual general meeting.

28 November — Christmas at the park

Alsatian Christmas traditions, craft stalls, food and evening dinner.
