Volunteer workcamps

International heritage workcamps in Reichstett for volunteers aged 16 and over.

Who can join?

Everyone aged 16 or over. No previous experience is required and all nationalities are welcome.

Summer sessions

16–28 July or 31 July–12 August. Work takes place from 9 am–12 pm and 2–5 pm.

What will we do?

Timber framing, lime masonry, cob, roofing, painting, archaeology, gardens and restoration of rural objects.

Accommodation

Accommodation and washing facilities are provided by Reichstett. Bring a sleeping bag, mattress, sturdy clothes and swimwear.
