Plan your visit

Visit the park

Discover the Alsatian House Park freely or with a guide. Groups and schools are welcome by appointment.

Opening hours

From 1 April to mid-October: Wednesday and Thursday, 2–5 pm; even-numbered Sundays, 2–6 pm. Closed during French school holidays.

Admission

€2.50 for visitors over 14; €2 per person for groups over 20; €3 per pupil, including one activity.

Schools

Discovery trails, rural life and naturally coloured Easter eggs introduce children to Alsatian heritage.

Getting here

Rue du Terrain, 67116 Reichstett, after the stork park and about 10 km north of Strasbourg.
