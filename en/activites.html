Learn by doing

Association activities

Heritage skills, traditions and shared experiences.

Traditional skills

Lime masonry, cob, carpentry, roofing, rendering, furniture restoration and gardening.

Alsatian traditions

Easter eggs, wood-fired tarte flambée, regional crafts and celebrations throughout the seasons.

Discoveries

Visits to Strasbourg, the Black Forest, castles, vineyards, museums and thermal baths.

Local volunteering

Local volunteers meet on Wednesday and Thursday afternoons outside school holidays.
